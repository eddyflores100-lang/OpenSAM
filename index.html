The problem: 30,000+ notices a month

SAM.gov publishes thousands of opportunities weekly. Reading them manually is impossible, and filtering by NAICS says nothing about whether a contract fits your company.

The method: fit × probability

An explainable engine scores fit; a calibrated model estimates win probability. You work the shortlist where you have a real edge — with the math to defend it.

The discipline: pipeline + rotation

Every pursuit lives in the pipeline from discovery to award, and your API key rotation is tracked to the day — the scan never dies on an expired credential.

Everything OpenSAM does

Six modules. One goal: bid where you have an unfair advantage.

Affinity engine

Every SAM.gov notice is scored 0–100 against your company: NAICS codes, capabilities, target agencies, geography, eligibility, and size. Stop reading thousands of notices — work the ones that fit.

Win-probability model

A calibrated logistic model estimates your odds per contract — and every prediction is explainable, factor by factor. No black boxes.

Live scanner

Plug in your free api.data.gov key and scan real opportunities as they publish. No key? Demo mode shows the full workflow with sample data.

Capture pipeline

Drag pursuits from New to Won. Notes, frozen predictions, and stage counts — everything persists on your device.

AI analysis per contract

A structured brief in seconds: fit, risks, and a proposal playbook — written from the same explainable factors, not vibes.

90-day key vault

Your API key lives encrypted on your own disk (or browser storage). OpenSAM counts the days and tells you when to rotate.
