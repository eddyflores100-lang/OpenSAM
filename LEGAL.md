All federal opportunity data displayed or retrieved by OpenSAM comes from the [SAM.gov Opportunities Public API](https://open.gsa.gov/api/get-opportunities-public-api/), which is publicly accessible to anyone with a free api.data.gov key. OpenSAM does not cache, store, or redistribute the underlying federal data beyond what is necessary to display search results in real time.
