Acceptable Use Policy

What you may and may not do with OpenSAM. Short, specific, enforceable.

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Last updated: September 16, 2026 · Operator: AliceLabs LLC · Jurisdiction: United States

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) governs your use of OpenSAM (the “Service”) operated by AliceLabs LLC. It is incorporated by reference into our Terms of Service. Capitalized terms have the meanings given in the Terms.

1. Permitted uses

You may use the Service to:

  • Search, filter, and score federal contracting opportunities published on SAM.gov.
  • Build AI agents that interact with SAM.gov data through the typed SDK.
  • Build internal tools, dashboards, and reports for your own business development.
  • Fork, modify, and distribute the open-source packages under the MIT License.
  • Build commercial products and services on top of the open-source packages.
  • Submit bids on SAM.gov opportunities using information you derived from the Service.

2. Prohibited uses

You may not use the Service to:

  • Submit fraudulent, deceptive, or anti-competitive bids on SAM.gov, including bids that misrepresent your company’s capabilities, certifications, or past performance.
  • Redistribute SAM.gov opportunity data for resale without adding material value. Bulk-downloading opportunities and reselling the feed is prohibited; building a product that uses the data is permitted.
  • Evade rate limits by creating multiple api.data.gov accounts, multiple OpenSAM accounts, or rotating IPs.
  • Scrape or crawl the opensam.us hosted infrastructure, including the hosted API. Use the documented REST endpoints, not HTML scraping.
  • Reverse-engineer the hosted infrastructure, decompile the proprietary components, or attempt to extract source code that is not published under the MIT License.
  • Interfere with the Service’s operation, including denial-of-service attacks, port scanning, or attempting to gain unauthorized access to AliceLabs’s systems.
  • Build a directly competing product that repackages the Service without adding substantial new functionality, without written permission from AliceLabs.
  • Use the Service to develop, train, or evaluate AI models intended to compete with OpenSAM, without written permission.
  • Use the Service in violation of any law, including U.S. export controls, sanctions, or anti-bribery laws.
  • Use the Service to harass, defame, or impersonate any person or entity.

3. SAM.gov data rules

You acknowledge that:

  • SAM.gov opportunity data is provided by the U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) under a public API.
  • The SAM.gov API Terms of Use govern your use of the underlying data.
  • You are solely responsible for compliance with SAM.gov’s terms, including any limits on commercial use of the data.
  • AliceLabs is not responsible for the accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of SAM.gov data.

4. AI and automated agents

If you build AI agents that use the Service, you are responsible for the actions of those agents. You must:

  • Implement rate limiting in your agent to stay within your plan’s limits.
  • Identify your agent in the User-Agent header with a contact email.
  • Not use the agent to submit automated bids on SAM.gov without human review.
  • Not use the agent to train a competing AI model without written permission.

5. Enforcement

We may suspend or terminate your access to the Service immediately if we believe you have violated this AUP. We will notify you by email with the reason for suspension. For first-time, non-willful violations, we may issue a warning instead of suspending. For willful or repeated violations, we may terminate your account and refuse future service.

6. Reporting violations

If you become aware of a violation of this AUP, please report it to contacto@alicelabs.site with subject “AUP violation.” We investigate all reports and may take action including suspension, termination, or referral to law enforcement.

7. Changes to this AUP

We may update this AUP. We will notify Pro and Enterprise subscribers by email at least 14 days before material changes take effect.
