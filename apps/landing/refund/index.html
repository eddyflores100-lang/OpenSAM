Refund Policy

If the Service did not work for you, we will give your money back. No theater.

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Last updated: September 16, 2026 · Operator: AliceLabs LLC · Jurisdiction: United States

We want you to pay for OpenSAM only if it works for you. This Refund Policy explains when and how we refund paid subscriptions.

1. The Free plan

The Free plan costs $0. There is nothing to refund.

2. Pro plan — monthly

If you cancel within the first 30 days of your first monthly subscription, we will refund 100% of the amount paid, no questions asked. Email contacto@alicelabs.site with subject “Refund — Pro” and the email of your account.

After the first 30 days, monthly subscriptions are non-refundable. You may cancel at any time and your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

3. Pro plan — annual

Annual Pro subscriptions are refundable on a pro-rated basis. If you cancel after 90 days of an annual subscription, we will refund the unused portion, calculated as (days remaining ÷ 365) × (annual price paid), minus a $25 processing fee. Email contacto@alicelabs.site to request.

4. Enterprise plan

Enterprise plans are governed by the master agreement signed at the start of the term. Standard terms: annual, non-refundable, with service credits for SLA breach (see below). Custom refund terms may be negotiated per contract.

5. Service credits (SLA breach)

If we fail to meet the Enterprise SLA (99.9% monthly uptime), you are eligible for service credits as follows:

  • Uptime below 99.9% but at or above 99.0%: 10% of monthly fee as credit
  • Uptime below 99.0% but at or above 95.0%: 25% of monthly fee as credit
  • Uptime below 95.0%: 50% of monthly fee as credit

To claim service credits, email contacto@alicelabs.site within 30 days of the incident. Credits apply to future invoices and are not paid in cash.

6. How refunds are processed

Refunds are processed back to the original payment method via Stripe within 7 business days. Bank processing times may take an additional 3–10 days. We do not charge refund fees on monthly Pro; the $25 fee applies only to annual Pro pro-rated refunds.

7. Account termination

If we terminate your account for cause (violation of our Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy), no refund is owed. If we terminate for our own business reasons (e.g., discontinuing the Service), we will refund the pro-rated unused portion of your subscription.

8. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is unauthorized or incorrect, please contact us first at contacto@alicelabs.site before initiating a chargeback with your bank. We will resolve the issue within 5 business days. If we cannot resolve it, we will not contest a chargeback.

9. Currency

All prices and refunds are in US Dollars (USD). If you paid in another currency via your bank’s conversion, the refund amount will be the USD equivalent at the original transaction’s exchange rate, less any foreign-exchange fees your bank charges.
