Know exactly what the pilot covers.

The current tool requests the first 25 notices posted in the last 30 days and displays active records from that page. It does not exhaust the market, read attachments or determine eligibility.

Matching adds 50 points for NAICS alignment and 10 per distinct capability phrase found in the title, up to 50. It does not estimate your probability of winning.
