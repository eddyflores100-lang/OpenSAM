Privacy Policy

What we collect, what we keep, what we sell (nothing). Written in English, not legalese.

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Last updated: September 16, 2026 · Operator: AliceLabs LLC · Jurisdiction: United States

This Privacy Policy describes how AliceLabs LLC (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use OpenSAM (the “Service”). We wrote this in plain English. If anything here conflicts with our Terms of Service, the Terms control.

1. What we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information — email address, company name, and (for Pro/Enterprise) billing details. We use Stripe for payment processing and never see or store your full card number.
  • Company profile — NAICS codes, certifications, capabilities you configure for scoring. This is the data we use to score opportunities.
  • Communications — emails you send us, support tickets, and feedback.

Information we collect automatically

  • Usage data — which features you use, queries you run, opportunities you view. We use this to improve the Service.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, operating system, referral URL. Used for security, abuse prevention, and analytics.
  • Cookies — we use first-party cookies for session authentication and CSRF protection. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. See Section 6 below.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not run analytics that profile you across other websites.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information. Ever.
  • We do not add telemetry to the open-source npm packages. The packages do not phone home.
  • We do not collect biometric data, precise geolocation, or health information.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the Service — scoring opportunities, sending digests, firing webhooks.
  • To communicate with you — account updates, security alerts, support responses.
  • To improve the Service — aggregated analytics, error tracking, performance monitoring.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse — rate-limit evasion, scraping, account sharing.
  • To comply with legal obligations — subpoenas, court orders, law-enforcement requests.

3. Legal bases for processing (GDPR/UK GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to provide the Service you requested.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — security, fraud prevention, and product improvement, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for optional marketing emails and non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — to comply with US and applicable law.

5. Sharing

We share personal data only in these circumstances:

  • Service providers — Stripe (payment), AWS (hosting), transactional email providers. All under written contracts prohibiting further sharing.
  • Legal compliance — in response to a valid subpoena, court order, or government request, when we believe disclosure is required by law.
  • Business transfer — if AliceLabs is acquired or merges, we will notify you 30 days before any data transfer and the recipient must honor this Privacy Policy.
  • Aggregate or de-identified data — we may share aggregated statistics that cannot identify you.

We do not share data with data brokers. We do not share data for advertising.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Restriction — request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent — for processing based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email contacto@alicelabs.site with subject “Privacy request.” We respond within 30 days. We do not charge a fee unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

US state privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA and similar)

If you are a California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, or Texas resident, you have additional rights under state law, including the right to know what personal data we collect, the right to opt out of “sales” or “sharing” (we do not sell or share), and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures: TLS 1.3 for transit, AES-256 for data at rest, scoped IAM roles, regular dependency audits, and annual penetration testing. We never store full payment card numbers (Stripe handles that). For security incidents, see our Security page for the responsible disclosure process.

9. International transfers

If you access the Service from outside the United States, your data will be processed in the United States. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for transfers from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland. By using the Service, you consent to these transfers.

10. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify Pro and Enterprise subscribers by email at least 30 days before material changes take effect. The “Last updated” date above always reflects the current version.
