How the current pilot handles data.

The public landing has no contact form, analytics script or account system. Email links open your email application. Any message you send is handled through email; avoid secrets and sensitive proposal content.

In the private search app, your title query, optional NAICS and capability phrases go to the pilot server. Only title, NAICS, date filters and the server provider key are sent to SAM.gov. Capability matching happens on the pilot server.

The access code stays in page memory and is cleared on reload. The application does not implement a search-history database. Hosting, reverse-proxy and email providers may keep operational records under their configuration.

Before a hosted engagement, confirm the actual operator, infrastructure, retention and handling requirements in the service agreement. Contact AliceLabs for data-handling questions.

OpenSAM is an AliceLabs project. Not affiliated with the U.S. Government, GSA or SAM.gov.
