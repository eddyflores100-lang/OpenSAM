Section C5 · Legal Affairs

Data Processing Agreement

The standard DPA for Enterprise customers. Sign it as-is, or use it as a starting point for negotiation.

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Last updated: September 16, 2026 · Operator: AliceLabs LLC · Jurisdiction: United States

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) is entered into by and between AliceLabs LLC (“Processor”) and the customer entity identified in the signed order form (“Controller”). This DPA is incorporated into the master agreement between the parties and is effective as of the date of the order form.

1. Definitions

Capitalized terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the GDPR, the CCPA, or the master agreement, in that order. “Personal Data” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed by Processor on behalf of Controller under the master agreement.

2. Roles and scope

The parties acknowledge and agree that Controller is the controller (or processor, where applicable) of Personal Data and Processor is the processor (or sub-processor, where applicable) acting on Controller’s documented instructions. The scope, nature, and purpose of processing, the types of Personal Data, and the categories of data subjects are described in our Privacy Policy and the order form.

3. Processor obligations

Processor shall:

  • Process Personal Data only on Controller’s documented instructions, including with regard to transfers of Personal Data to a third country, unless required by law.
  • Ensure that personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, as described in Section 5 below.
  • Assist Controller in responding to data subject requests, where technically feasible.
  • Assist Controller in meeting Controller’s obligations under Articles 32 to 36 of the GDPR (security, breach notification, DPIA, prior consultation).
  • Delete or return all Personal Data to Controller after the end of the services, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Make available to Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and allow for and contribute to audits.

4. Sub-processors

Controller grants Processor general authorization to engage sub-processors, provided that Processor:

  • Enters into a written agreement with the sub-processor imposing the same data protection obligations as in this DPA.
  • Remains liable to Controller for the sub-processor’s performance.
  • Notifies Controller of intended changes to sub-processors at least 30 days in advance, giving Controller the right to object.

Current sub-processors: Amazon Web Services, Inc. (hosting); Stripe, Inc. (payment processing). A complete and current list is maintained at opensam.us/dpa#subprocessors.

5. Security measures

Processor implements the following technical and organizational measures:

  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.3 for all data in motion.
  • Encryption at rest: AES-256 for all stored data.
  • Access controls: Role-based access, least-privilege, MFA for all administrative access.
  • Audit logging: All access to Personal Data is logged and retained for 24 months.
  • Network security: VPC isolation, security groups, no public inbound access to databases.
  • Vulnerability management: Continuous dependency scanning, annual penetration testing.
  • Incident response: Documented IR plan with 24-hour notification to Controller for confirmed breaches.
  • Personnel: Background checks for personnel with access to Personal Data, annual security training.

6. Personal data breach

Processor shall notify Controller of a confirmed Personal Data breach without undue delay, and in any case within 24 hours of becoming aware of the breach. Notification shall include: (a) the nature of the breach; (b) the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned; (c) the likely consequences; (d) the measures taken or proposed. Processor shall cooperate with Controller in investigating and mitigating the breach.

7. International transfers

For transfers of Personal Data from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to the United States, Processor relies on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914) and the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, as applicable. Processor has self-certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (if applicable) and adheres to its principles.

8. Audit rights

Controller may audit Processor’s compliance with this DPA once per year, upon 30 days’ written notice, during business hours, and in a manner that does not disrupt Processor’s operations. Controller may also request a copy of Processor’s most recent SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent, which Processor shall provide under NDA.

9. Deletion on termination

Upon termination of the master agreement, Processor shall, at Controller’s choice, delete or return all Personal Data within 90 days, except where retention is required by law. Processor shall provide written confirmation of deletion upon request.

10. CCPA / CPRA

To the extent Processor processes Personal Information (as defined by the CCPA) on behalf of Controller, Processor certifies that it: (a) processes Personal Information as a service provider under CCPA; (b) does not sell or share Personal Information; (c) does not use Personal Information for purposes other than providing the services; (d) will notify Controller if Processor determines it can no longer meet its CCPA obligations.
